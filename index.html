Editing keys

← → or PgUp PgDnPrevious / next slide, with nothing selected
T · AAdd a text box · open Add, in the left panel
Enter · EscType in the selected text box · done, then let go of the selection
⌘+B I UBold, italic, underline the selected text box
↑ ↓ ← → · with ⇧Nudge the selected item · ten times as far
⌥+↑ ↓Move the selected item one place earlier or later
⌘+C · ⌘+V · ⌘+DCopy · paste · duplicate the selected item (copy and paste the slide when nothing is selected)
DeleteDelete the selected item
⌘+Z · ⇧+⌘+Z or ⌘+YUndo · redo
⌘+S · ⌘+ESave to the Library · switch between the Lesson and Quiz studios
⌘+EnterPresent from this slide
⌘+KFind any command
? · EscShow this list · close it

Home EndFirst / last slide (not in a room)

OEvery slide at once — click one to go there

NSpeaker notes
FToggle full screen

VAsk the room a quick poll (Host live)
TTurn the room’s reactions on or off (Host live)
